<b>Stash</b> is a privacy-first, offline personal finance and lending tracker built with modern Android Jetpack Compose and Material 3 Dynamic Theming (Monet).

Track daily expenses, incomes, bank-cash transfers, and peer-to-peer loans without internet access, third-party analytics, accounts, or telemetry.

<b>Key Features:</b>
• <b>100% Offline & Private</b>: Zero network permissions, zero tracking, zero ads. Your financial records stay exclusively on your device.
• <b>Dual-Wallet Architecture</b>: Distinguish and balance Online Money (bank accounts) and Cash Available, with zero-distortion internal transfers.
• <b>Lent Money & Debt Manager</b>: Track loans given to friends and family with due dates, direct Call/WhatsApp actions, and partial repayment logging.
• <b>Native Keyboard Input</b>: Fast decimal numeric input using your device's software keyboard.
• <b>Fluid 120Hz Animations</b>: Hardware-accelerated transitions and responsive charts optimized for LTPO displays.
• <b>Biometric Protection</b>: Lock your sensitive ledger with fingerprint or device lock hardware authentication.
• <b>Export & Restore</b>: Full control of your financial records with encrypted JSON backups and CSV exports for spreadsheet analysis.
• <b>Dynamic Theming</b>: Seamlessly integrates with Android 12–15 Monet wallpaper colors in light and dark modes.
