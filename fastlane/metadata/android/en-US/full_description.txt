<b>Stash</b> is a privacy-first, offline personal finance and lending tracker built with modern Android Jetpack Compose and Material 3 Dynamic Theming (Monet).

Track daily expenses, incomes, bank-cash transfers, and peer-to-peer loans without internet access, third-party analytics, accounts, or telemetry.

<b>Key Features:</b>
• <b>100% Offline & Private</b>: Zero network permissions, zero tracking, zero ads. Your financial records stay exclusively on your device.
• <b>Dual-Wallet Architecture</b>: Distinguish and balance Online Money (bank accounts) and Cash Available, with zero-distortion internal transfers.
• <b>Low Balance Warnings</b>: Configurable balance threshold warnings for Online and Cash wallets with visual dashboard alerts and expense overspending checks.
• <b>Printable PDF Statements</b>: Generate clean, A4 financial statements with period summaries and categorized transaction ledgers ready to print or share.
• <b>Subscriptions Tracker</b>: Monitor recurring monthly services with due dates, monthly burn rate totals, and one-tap payment logging.
• <b>Home Screen Widget</b>: Live Online and Cash balance glances with quick-action shortcuts to add income, spend, or transfer funds.
• <b>Lent Money & Debt Manager</b>: Track loans given to friends and family with due dates, direct communication actions, and partial repayment logging.
• <b>Biometric Protection</b>: Lock your sensitive financial ledger with biometric or device lock hardware authentication.
• <b>Export & Restore</b>: Full control of your records with local JSON backups and CSV exports for external spreadsheet analysis.
• <b>Dynamic Material 3</b>: Fluid animations and dynamic Monet wallpaper-based theming in light and dark modes.
