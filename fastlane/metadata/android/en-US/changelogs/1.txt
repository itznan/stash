Initial release of Stash (v1.0.0):
• 100% offline personal finance and dual-wallet tracking (Bank/UPI and Cash)
• Peer-to-peer loan and debt manager with partial repayment logging and reminders
• Fast decimal keyboard input and custom tag classification
• Material 3 dynamic theming (Monet) with dark and light mode
• Biometric authentication (fingerprint/device lock)
• Local encrypted JSON backup and CSV export/import
• Zero internet permissions, zero analytics, zero ads
